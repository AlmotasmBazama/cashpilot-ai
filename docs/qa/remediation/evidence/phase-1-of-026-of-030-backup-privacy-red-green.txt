Verified local contracts
------------------------

  types, including the safe settings marker; a second unchanged sync uploads
  no document.
- Snapshot completeness/version are written only after all pending documents
  and metadata succeed.
- Consent is checked at execution start and at every awaited write boundary.
  Generation changes invalidate pre-disable work; disable drains writers and
  purges the scoped queue before returning.

  amount, note, account, or other prior fields.
