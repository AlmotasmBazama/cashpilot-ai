# CashPilot AI Brand System

The official CashPilot AI brand source is `assets/brand/cashpilot_ai_official_source.png`. All launcher, splash, favicon, monochrome, transparent, and in-app brand assets are cropped or masked derivatives of that single supplied image. Do not redraw, reinterpret, or replace the logo from another source.
